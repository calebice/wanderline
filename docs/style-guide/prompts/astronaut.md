# Astronaut comparison asset prompts

These notes document the approved original astronaut comparison set. The images use
one recurring idea—a relaxed anonymous astronaut in a roomy, ruffled pressure suit—
while allowing each drawing language to choose its own pose, viewpoint, composition,
and space environment. No artist, studio, production, agency, or copyrighted
character was used as a visual anchor.

## Shared direction

Create a 3:2 learner-friendly illustration featuring one generic astronaut with an
opaque visor and an oversized, snowboard-outfit-like suit. Retain broad fabric
bunching at the shoulders, elbows, waist, knees, and ankles. The astronaut should
feel laid-back and delighted to be among the stars rather than stiff or heroic.
Include no face, logo, flag, mission patch, brand, readable text, watermark, or
recognizable space-agency equipment.

## Approved realism reference

Stage a low three-quarter view of the astronaut reclining against the rounded lip of
a shallow lunar crater, with one boot close to the viewer and a softly glowing planet
arc in the distance. Use observed weight, natural proportions, convincing fabric
compression, padded seams, gradual cool-gray values, restrained reflected light,
and selective lunar texture. Keep the finish illustrative rather than photographic.

## Approved cartoon reference

Float the astronaut diagonally through open space with both hands tucked behind the
helmet, bent knees, and chunky boots kicked toward the viewer. Build the figure from
a large round helmet, broad marshmallow torso, oversized extremities, rhythmic fold
bands, bold variable contours, flat warm colors, and graphic three-value shadows.
Use a few rounded asteroids and simple star shapes to reinforce the playful motion.

## Approved architectural reference

Perch the astronaut casually on a tall fractured moon-rock column, seen from a
slightly low side angle, with one leg hanging and one knee raised. Emphasize the
vertical drop using disciplined line-weight hierarchy, clear cylinders and suit
modules, light perspective-aware construction traces, crisp rock planes, and pale
cool-gray and ochre marker washes. Include no labels, measurements, arrows, or grid.

## Approved watercolor reference

Place the astronaut along a gently curved lunar ridge in a wide side view, with one
knee raised and the body relaxed beneath a large indigo-violet sky. Use transparent
washes, visible warm paper, reserved suit highlights, blue-violet shadows, soft
granulating rock, wet-on-wet blooms, glazing, lost-and-found edges, scattered stars,
and a softly glowing planet. Keep dark accents sparse and folds flowing.

## Approved anime-environment reference

Use an original cinematic rear three-quarter view of the astronaut sitting on an
asteroid rim with both legs dangling and straight arms supporting the body. Make the
figure smaller in frame while an immense ringed planet and luminous blue star field
carry the wonder of the scene. Organize the suit into bold cel-like shapes with crisp
shadow groups, clean major edges, selective painted atmosphere, and warm rim light.

## Approval history

- The initial shared seated pose and oversized ruffled suit were approved as the
  neutral character direction.
- A first pose-matched five-style set was accepted as a useful study, but the user
  asked for more creative staging so the styles would not read as filters.
- The second set introduced an independent pose, viewpoint, and background for every
  drawing language while preserving the relaxed astronaut theme.
- The watercolor treatment was identified as the favorite visual direction and the
  complete second set was approved for the application.
