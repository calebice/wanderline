# Greenhouse comparison asset prompts

These notes document the approved original greenhouse-after-rain comparison set.
The shared idea is a compact dark green or teal glasshouse with a pitched roof,
warm interior light, wet garden reflections, a simple chair, a potting table, and
three readable plant masses. Each drawing language independently chooses its
viewpoint and emphasis. No artist, studio, production, or existing artwork was
used as an anchor.

## Approved realism reference

Use an eye-level three-quarter view along a wet garden path. Describe rain-beaded
glass, painted metal, damp wood, foliage, and reflections with truthful perspective,
naturalistic colored-pencil texture, and selective detail. Contrast a cool gray-green
garden with the warm interior.

## Approved cartoon reference

Use a playful low three-quarter view and a squat greenhouse with oversized roof
panes and an arched doorway. Build the garden from large plant shapes, rounded
stepping stones, and puddles. Use bold variable contour, flat color, and three-value
graphic shadows.

## Approved architectural reference

Use an elevated axonometric view that reveals roof pitch, mullions, door, shelves,
chair, potting bench, circulation, and paving. Use disciplined line weights,
accurate construction, restrained marker wash, and sparse contextual planting,
without labels, dimensions, grids, arrows, or callouts.

## Approved watercolor reference

Set the greenhouse off-center beyond a curving wet path with generous breathing
room. Use transparent cool blue-green washes, warm amber light through glass,
granulation, blooms, reserved paper, and lost-and-found edges. Keep furniture and
plant masses simple enough for a newer artist to study.

## Approved anime-environment reference

Use an original cinematic wide view at blue hour. Nest the smaller greenhouse
inside dark wet foreground foliage and a layered garden, making its amber interior
the focal point. Combine crisp greenhouse construction with atmospheric indigo and
teal planting and broad pavement reflections. Do not imitate or name any artist,
studio, production, character, or signature motif.

## Approval history

- The five images were generated together as one efficient preview round.
- The user approved the entire set without revisions.
- The watercolor thumbnail represents the subject in the selector because its
  luminous rainy atmosphere most clearly expresses the collection's shared idea.
