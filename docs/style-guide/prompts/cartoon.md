# Cartoon asset prompts

These prompts document approved original assets. Source names and existing artwork
were intentionally excluded from image-generation instructions.

## Current guide showcase: flat shape design with selective ink character

Use case: stylized-concept followed by a targeted image edit

Asset type: reference for an educational drawing-style guide

Create an original bold, flat, shape-driven Cartoon interpretation of a wholly
fictional Japanese seaside town. Show a coastal hillside with a small cluster of
Japanese homes, dark ceramic tiled roofs, a narrow stair lane descending toward a
harbor and turquoise sea, a few logically connected utility poles, retaining walls,
and dense greenery.

Use a 3:2 elevated three-quarter composition with the stair as a broad curved path.
Compress hillside depth so roofs stack rhythmically, and simplify the town into six
to nine large interlocking shapes. Favor rounded friendly arcs in roofs, foliage,
clouds, coastline, and stair turns, contrasted with sturdy rectangular walls and
poles. Enlarge the stair and foreground roof shapes and reduce incidental buildings.

Use opaque flat color, controlled warm-indigo contours, four value groups, graphic
shadows, and a limited palette of cream, coral, indigo, turquoise, golden green, and
peach. Retain only identifying roof rhythms, window groups, stair divisions, sparse
wire arcs, and clustered leaf scallops. Slightly exaggerate roof curves, stair turns,
and foliage volume while keeping architecture coherent.

In the approved revision, preserve that composition and broad graphic design while
replacing sterile edges with subtly varied hand-drawn contours. Add small intentional
irregularities to foreground roof curves, stone rhythm, stair divisions, windows, and
foliage. Limit expressive ink texture to a few eave marks, stone hatch groups, and
leaf-edge strokes near the stair. Strengthen the coral accent and local indigo shadow
at the stair turn. Keep the balance approximately eighty percent clean flat animation
illustration and twenty percent expressive hand-inked character.

Create exactly one finished landscape scene. Include no people, animals, characters,
vehicles, readable signage, text, labels, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Do not
add random props or distort structural connections. Avoid anime conventions,
cinematic painting, watercolor, dense hatching, photorealism, 3D-rendered polish,
uniform black outlines, copied locations, malformed buildings, and studio-specific
motifs.

## Approved learner reference

Create a simpler companion to the Cartoon showcase using the same palette, rounded
shape rhythm, warm-indigo contours, and restrained handmade accents. Show two
principal house masses, one broad S-shaped stair, one curved retaining wall, one
pole with three wire arcs, three foliage groups, an open sea, and one distant
headland. Organize the composition into six dominant silhouettes and four value
groups. Retain one coral focal accent and only a few eave, stone, and leaf marks.
Keep the scene constructible from silhouettes, boxes, roof wedges, flat color, and a
handful of final accents. Include no people, text, realistic modeling, dense texture,
or additional architectural complexity.

## Approved learner process sheet

Create four equal panels in a 2-by-2 grid using the exact learner-reference crop.
Panel one shows only six flat dominant silhouettes. Panel two ghosts those shapes
and adds house boxes, roof wedges, horizon, compressed depth guides, the exaggerated
S stair, wall thickness, pole, wires, and foliage envelopes. Panel three keeps the
structure faint while adding four flat value groups and the limited cream, peach,
indigo, turquoise, green, and coral palette. Panel four adds only controlled contour
variation, roof and stair divisions, simple windows, wires, wall marks, and sparse
leaf accents. Keep the sequence cumulative, introduce no new objects, and embed no
text or tutorial chrome.
