# Camper van comparison asset prompts

These notes document the approved original camper-van comparison set. Every image
uses a compact generic cream-and-sage pop-top van, open side door, and nearby folding
chair. Each drawing language controls its own viewpoint, landscape, and mood so the
results demonstrate more than a surface filter. No artist, studio, vehicle maker,
real destination, or existing artwork was used as an anchor.

## Approved realism reference

Show the van from a natural eye-level three-quarter view on a textured mountain
overlook at golden hour. Model the wheels, body, pop-top fabric, glass, rubber,
interior shadow, gravel, grasses, and distant valley with believable perspective,
gradual values, selective edges, and restrained cream, sage, ochre, blue-gray, and
sunset color. Keep the front panel unbranded and free of text-like marks.

## Approved cartoon reference

Stage a playful low three-quarter coastal view. Push the van into a squat, friendly
silhouette with a taller pop-top, oversized wheels, chunky bumper, curved windows,
bold variable contours, flat color, and three-value graphic shadows. Simplify the
hills, coast, clouds, and grass into recurring shapes, with the folding chair as a
small orange accent.

## Approved architectural reference

Present an elevated three-quarter design view on a mostly paper-colored overlook
plane. Explain the van through accurate wheel ellipses, box-and-cylinder construction,
modular windows and doors, open-door circulation, visible interior units, strong
profile lines, lighter construction traces, and restrained gray and sage washes.
Include no labels, dimensions, arrows, or grid.

## Approved watercolor reference

Place the van off-center at a broad coastal overlook near sunrise, with the open door
and chair facing the water. Give more than half the image to luminous sky and sea.
Use visible cold-pressed paper, reserved whites, transparent cobalt, turquoise,
lavender, sage, and earth washes, soft distant headlands, granulation, blooms,
glazing, lost edges, and only a few crisp accents around the van and chair.

## Approved anime-environment reference

Use an original cinematic rear three-quarter view at blue hour. Keep the camper small
within a high forest turnout overlooking a winding river valley, layered mountains,
and first stars. Contrast crisp cel-like van shapes and a warm open-door pool of
light with painterly trees and atmospheric distance. Do not reference or imitate a
named artist, studio, production, character, or signature motif.

## Approval history

- The user selected a camper van as the next subject because it bridges structured
  perspective drawing and environmental storytelling.
- The initial cartoon output was rejected internally because it became a text-heavy
  tutorial poster; it was replaced with a single uninterrupted scenic illustration.
- Badge-like pseudo-lettering on the realism van was removed while preserving the
  approved composition and rendering.
- The five cleaned scenic references were approved together for the application.
