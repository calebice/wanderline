# Anime environment asset prompts

These prompts document approved original assets. Source, artist, studio, production,
and existing-work names were intentionally excluded from image-generation
instructions.

## Current guide showcase: graphic cliffside production background

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: reference for an educational drawing-style guide

Create an original Japanese 2D animation environment background of a wholly
fictional seaside hillside town. Use crisp hand-drawn shape edges, graphic cel-painted
value grouping, dramatic vertical scale, layered rock and foliage silhouettes, deep
cool shadows, bright sunlit greens, simplified material planes, and a clearly
illustrated production-background finish. Do not imitate a named artist, studio,
film, series, or existing production.

Show a rugged coastal headland rising from a blue-green cove. Arrange six to eight
separate plausible tiled-roof homes across three broad man-made terraces, connected
by a long zigzag public stair descending to a small waterfront landing. Include
stone retaining walls, two connected utility poles, dense windswept trees, and sea
glimpses. Use a modest drainage spillway only as infrastructure; include no tall
waterfall, central fantasy building, floating structure, or copied cliff silhouette.

Use a 3:2 elevated three-quarter camera. Place the tallest rock-and-tree mass on one
side and open sea on the other, with the town stepping diagonally between them.
Organize dark foreground foliage, focal middle terraces, and pale sea/distant coast
as distinct production layers. Emphasize verticality through stacked terrace bands,
narrow stair flights, tree trunks, and descending walls while retaining one clean
staging area on a landing.

Use clean opaque color shapes, selective dark-teal structural accents, simplified
faceted rocks, broad tree canopies, restrained roof rhythms, and hard-edged cast
shadows. Organize five values from near-black teal foreground through blue-green
shade, middle ochre/olive, bright yellow-green and cream light, to pale cyan distance.
Keep strongest contrast at the terrace-and-stair junction and reduce detail and
contrast with distance.

Use clear late-morning coastal sun and a limited deep-teal, blue-green, slate-blue,
ochre, cedar-orange, yellow-green, and pale-cyan palette. Include only restrained
planters, plaster repairs, drainage, covered landing, and utility hardware. Include
no characters, vehicles, boats, text, signs, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Avoid
copied motifs, fantasy architecture, named-studio imitation, watercolor,
photorealism, 3D rendering, depth-of-field blur, malformed buildings, tangled wires,
and indiscriminate detail.

## Approved learner reference

Create a compact companion using the showcase's crisp shape edges, opaque cel-painted
color, five-value design, deep teal shadows, bright yellow-green foliage, faceted
rock, and vertical staging. Reduce the site to three terraces, three separate roofed
homes, one continuous zigzag stair to a water landing, three retaining-wall bands,
one pole with three wire arcs, four canopy masses, one rock plane, and one distant
sea/headland shape. Use a dark foreground overlay, focal middle terraces, and pale
background. Keep five repeated value/color families and concentrate contrast at the
central landing. Omit drainage, extra buildings, detailed shoreline, dense planting,
and small infrastructure.

## Approved learner process sheet

Create four equal panels using the exact learner-reference scene and camera. Panel
one shows only the foreground, middle, and background production layers as five
grayscale value masses. Panel two ghosts those masses and adds the sloped site wedge,
three terraces, three house boxes and roofs, continuous stair, walls, rock plane,
pole, wires, canopy envelopes, horizon, and perspective guides. Panel three adds the
five opaque color-script groups and simple cast shadows while leaving the staging
landing clear. Panel four adds only structural accents, roof and stair divisions,
simple openings, wall joints, rock facets, sparse trunks and leaf edges, pole
hardware, and wires. Maintain identical staging and embed no text or tutorial chrome.
