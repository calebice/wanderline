# Bouquet comparison asset prompts

These notes document the approved original bouquet collection. Unlike the vehicle
and astronaut comparisons, the bouquet category intentionally changes both the
vessel and flowers for every drawing language. This lets each reference demonstrate
a distinct approach to silhouette, overlap, negative space, material, and setting.
No artist, studio, production, or existing artwork was used as an anchor.

## Approved realism reference

Use a tall smoky-teal glass bottle holding peach tulips, white narcissus, and spare
olive branches. Stage it at eye level on a dark walnut table beside cool window light.
Render believable glass thickness, water line, stem refraction, leaf curl, petal
translucency, natural weight, gradual values, and selective sharp focus.

## Approved cartoon reference

Use the selected earlier cartoon study: a high-angle fan of oversized coral peonies,
yellow daisy-like flowers, lavender spikes, and long eucalyptus branches in a squat
cream ceramic vase with a cobalt band. Build the arrangement from bold variable
contours, simple petal symbols, flat color, and graphic three-value shadows.

## Approved architectural reference

Use a tall faceted charcoal concrete vessel containing white calla lilies,
rust-orange pincushion protea, and blade-like leaves. Arrange the stems as sparse
directional axes on a pale stone plinth. Explain the forms with line-weight hierarchy,
fine construction traces, faceted planes, and restrained marker washes without
labels, dimensions, arrows, grids, or text.

## Approved watercolor reference

Use a translucent sea-green glass jar holding a wind-tossed meadow bouquet of red
poppies, pink cosmos, cobalt cornflowers, white lace-flower umbels, and fine grasses.
Place it low beside a rain-washed garden window. Preserve open paper, transparent
glazing, watery reflections, soft blooms, granulation, broken edges, and a few crisp
flower-center and jar-rim accents.

## Approved anime-environment reference

Use a tall ribbed terracotta vase holding orange tiger lilies, soft blue hydrangea
clusters, willow branches, and glossy leaves. Stage it near open balcony doors after
rain, with warm late-afternoon light, reflective floor planes, and a distant city.
Combine crisp focal shape groups with painterly environmental depth and do not imitate
or name any artist, studio, production, character, or signature motif.

## Approval history

- The initial set used one cream-and-blue vase and one recurring coral, yellow,
  lavender, and eucalyptus bouquet across all five styles.
- The user approved the rendering languages but requested greater variation in vase
  shapes, flower families, bouquet silhouettes, and arrangement logic.
- Four new varied references were approved: realism, architectural, watercolor, and
  anime environment.
- The new mustard-pitcher cartoon was not selected. The user chose the earlier
  cream-vase cartoon reference instead, and that image is the one shipped.
