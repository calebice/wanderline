# Watercolor asset prompts

These prompts document approved original assets. Source names and existing artwork
were intentionally excluded from image-generation instructions.

## Current guide showcase: atmospheric transparent watercolor

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: reference for an educational drawing-style guide

Create an original loose atmospheric transparent-watercolor interpretation of a
wholly fictional Japanese seaside hillside town. Show plausible small homes and
tiled roofs, a narrow stair descending toward a modest harbor and sea, connected
utility lines, retaining walls, and abundant greenery without depicting a
recognizable location.

Use a 3:2 elevated composition with the stair and roof cluster forming a diagonal
path toward a large open sea shape. Group the village into three or four connected
wash masses. Keep pale sky and sea spacious, define a few overlapping roof
silhouettes and stair landings, and use one dark pole as a spatial anchor.

Render traditional transparent watercolor on visible cold-pressed cotton paper.
Use broad wet-on-wet washes for sky, sea, distant land, and foliage; natural soft
merges, restrained blooms, tide edges, granulation, and color separation; large
reserved-paper passages for sunlit walls, steps, and water sparkle; and two or three
transparent glazes for selected roof and foreground shadows. Use no opaque white.

Allow soft or lost boundaries across distance and retain only a small number of crisp
wet-on-dry edges near the stair, roof ridges, pole, and foreground wall. Add sparse
dry-brush marks at the focal area. Organize three broad value families with cobalt,
turquoise, rose, ochre, indigo, sap and olive greens, plus a small burnt-sienna
accent. Reserve the darkest darks for connected marks near the stair and pole.

Create exactly one finished landscape. Include no people, characters, vehicles,
boats, text, labels, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Avoid anime, cartoon contours,
architectural guides, line-and-wash ink structure, photorealism, gouache opacity,
digital gradients, copied locations, all-over splatter, muddy color, malformed
buildings, and excessive detail.

## Approved learner reference

Create a simpler companion using the showcase's transparent wet-on-wet atmosphere,
visible cold-pressed paper, reserved whites, granulation, lost edges, and restrained
darks. Reduce the scene to two principal house masses, one broad stair, one wall,
one pole with faint wires, three foliage washes, one headland, and only a few distant
roof suggestions. Organize six major shapes and three value families. Use one pale
connected wash, three softly mingled foliage/distance washes, one or two transparent
glazes, and no more than six crisp focal edges. Preserve broad paper-rich sky, sea,
walls, stairs, and water glints. Include no ink outlines, opaque white, dense detail,
or added harbor complexity.

## Approved learner process sheet

Create four equal panels using the exact learner-reference crop. Panel one shows a
very light graphite plan of six envelopes, horizon, stair direction, roof wedges,
wall, pole, and reserved-paper lights with no pigment. Panel two retains the drawing
and adds only a pale connected first wash plus soft wet-on-wet headland and foliage.
Panel three adds one or two transparent mid-value glazes for roofs, shadow, foliage,
distance, and warm wall accents while preserving soft edges. Panel four adds only
the pole and wires, main roof ridges, one stair turn, one wall edge, sparse focal
marks, dry brush, granulation, and one controlled tide edge. Keep the sequence
cumulative and embed no text or tutorial chrome.
