# Architectural drawing asset prompts

These prompts document approved original assets. Source names and existing artwork
were intentionally excluded from image-generation instructions.

## Current guide showcase: colored site axonometric

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: reference for an educational drawing-style guide

Create an original precise colored axonometric architectural presentation of a
wholly fictional Japanese seaside hillside town. Show a self-contained cutaway block
of steep coastal terrain meeting a modest harbor and sea. Arrange six to eight
plausible small houses with tiled roofs along a narrow stair route descending from
the upper hillside to the waterfront. Include retaining walls, one branching lane,
a breakwater, two connected utility poles, grouped planting, and visible terrain
strata at the cut edges.

Use a consistent architectural axonometric with verticals vertical and two horizontal
site axes parallel throughout. Include no vanishing points, horizon, or perspective
convergence. Present the site at a high three-quarter angle so roof planes, wall
heights, stair runs, terrain terraces, harbor edge, and sea relationship can all be
compared. Keep the block seated on warm white drawing paper with generous margins.

Use precise hand-drafted technical pen and graphite with the strongest profile around
the site-block cut and outer silhouettes, medium building and circulation edges,
lighter roof and terrain divisions, and very-light material hatching. Apply restrained
dry-pencil or flat-marker system color: pale sand for terrain and walls, indigo roofs,
terracotta circulation, olive planting, turquoise water, and cool-gray cut strata and
shadows. Keep color subordinate to the linework and geometrically align simple cast
shadows.

Make footprints, roof ridges, eaves, openings, wall thicknesses, stair landings,
drainage, terrain terraces, harbor construction, poles, and connections plausible.
Simplify planting so it does not obscure circulation. Include no perspective
convergence, exploded components, labels, dimensions, title block, people, vehicles,
boats, text, logos, signatures, or watermarks. Avoid cartoon game-map styling,
watercolor, photographic rendering, copied sites, impossible stairs, malformed roofs,
and mixed projection systems.

## Approved learner reference

Create a compact companion using the same parallel projection, heavy terrain cut,
warm paper, line hierarchy, and restrained system colors. Reduce the site to one
prism, three terraces, three roofed house volumes, one continuous terracotta stair,
three retaining walls, one water plane, one pole, and four planting masses. Expose
three terrain-strata bands on one side and keep the stair visually continuous from
the upper terrace to the water. Use aligned prisms and roof wedges, four line weights,
and repeated sand, indigo, terracotta, olive, turquoise, and cool-gray colors. Omit
labels, dimensions, perspective convergence, dense material detail, and decorative
site complexity.

## Approved learner process sheet

Create four equal panels using the exact learner-reference site and projection.
Panel one shows only the site prism, heavy vertical cut, three terraces, water plane,
and a small unlabeled axis guide. Panel two ghosts that base and adds three house
boxes, roof wedges, retaining-wall thicknesses, one continuous stair ribbon, pole,
and wire directions in construction colors. Panel three adds the four-level line
hierarchy and repeated system colors, plus four simple planting envelopes. Panel four
adds only simple openings, restrained roof and wall divisions, sparse planting,
terrain hatching, water strokes, and pole details. Preserve parallel axes and
identical site relationships throughout; embed no text or tutorial chrome.
