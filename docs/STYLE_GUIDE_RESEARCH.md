# Curated Style Reference Guide research

Status: Reference
Authority: Visual research, synthesis, and approval history
Last reviewed: 2026-09-09

This document records the recurring visual principles used to develop Wanderline's
original style references. Sources are research inputs, not visual templates,
endorsements, or generation-prompt language. No source composition, location,
palette, character, or signature motif may be copied.

### Deferred differentiation pass

The first treatments distinguish line, color, texture, and finish, but still read
too much like filters applied to a common drawing. A future revision should make
each drawing language more structurally distinct while retaining the same subject
and broadly comparable viewpoint. Revisit style-specific silhouette design,
proportion and exaggeration, edge behavior, value grouping, perspective emphasis,
detail hierarchy, and native medium marks. Judge success at thumbnail size: each
style should be identifiable before texture or fine detail is inspected.

## Realism

Status: the colored-pencil candidate is approved as the Realism showcase reference,
with a simpler colored-pencil companion approved as its learner study. The graphite
candidate is also approved stylistically and preserved for a future
graphite/pencil-specific reference collection.

### Research set

| Reference | Source type | Relevant recurring principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [National Gallery: Realism](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/glossary/realism) | Museum glossary | Realism privileges subjects and appearances drawn from ordinary observed life rather than idealization. |
| [Getty: Rocky Observations](https://www.getty.edu/education/for_teachers/curricula/landscapes/lesson03.html) | Museum education | Slow looking supports convincing shape, form, space, surface texture, and material description. |
| [Proko: Measuring Techniques](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/measuring-techniques) | Popular drawing education | Compare relationships across the whole subject, establish the large envelope first, and delay detail until proportion and placement work. |
| [BYU-Idaho ART 110 fundamentals](https://courses.byui.edu/art110_new/art110/week01/basic.html) | University course material | Value, saturation, temperature, and edge changes can describe atmospheric distance. |
| [Humanities LibreTexts: Visual Characteristics of Atmospheric Perspective](https://human.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Art/Studio_Arts/Drawing_Perspectives_II_%28Kennedy%29/05%3A_Atmospheric_Perspective/5.04%3A_Visual_Characteristics_of_Atmospheric_Perspective) | Open studio-art textbook | Distant forms typically lose contrast, saturation, texture visibility, and edge specificity. |

The set spans museum, university/open-textbook, and popular drawing-education
sources. The product synthesis uses only principles that recur across sources.

### Style synthesis matrix

| Dimension | Synthesized guidance for this guide |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Choose a credible viewpoint, establish a clear visual path, and organize foreground, middle distance, and background before rendering. |
| Construction | Measure the large envelope and major relationships first; build architecture from simple planes and volumes before surface detail. |
| Perspective | Keep horizon, convergence, scale change, overlap, and atmospheric recession mutually consistent. Accuracy serves believable space rather than sterile perfection. |
| Line | Use line to investigate and clarify form. Vary pressure and specificity with material, lighting, depth, and focal priority instead of outlining every object equally. |
| Shape | Preserve observed silhouettes, negative spaces, angles, and proportional relationships. Avoid beautifying or exaggerating shapes by default. |
| Value | Group light and shadow coherently under one light source, retain a readable large-value pattern, and reserve the strongest contrast for useful focal or foreground structure. |
| Color | Start from observed local color, then account for illumination, reflected light, and atmosphere. Reduce contrast and chroma with distance without applying a fixed color filter. |
| Texture | Describe how surfaces differ through direction, scale, edge, and mark—not through uniform noise. Texture should follow form and diminish with distance. |
| Detail | Work general to specific. Detail confirms structure and focal hierarchy; it does not compensate for inaccurate proportion or perspective. |
| Medium | Let graphite retain visible hatching, pressure, and paper; let colored pencil retain layered strokes and optical color mixing. Do not disguise the image as a photograph. |
| Workflow | Observe, establish the envelope, verify relationships and perspective, group values, refine materials selectively, then recheck the whole image. |

### Approved directions

- The current Realism guide uses the immersive stair-level colored-pencil rendering
  with natural local color, coherent sunlight, damp materials, and atmospheric depth.
- Its learner study reduces the problem to two principal houses, one stair direction,
  one pole, broad depth planes, four value groups, and three selective textures. The
  showcase remains inspirational; the learner study carries the tutorial sequence.
- The elevated panoramic graphite observation—with tonal hierarchy, explicit pencil
  construction, and reduced distant detail—is reserved intact for a future
  graphite/pencil-specific collection.

## Cartoon

Status: the clean flat-color candidate, revised with restrained hand-inked character,
is approved as the Cartoon showcase. Its simplified learner reference and cumulative
four-stage process sheet are also approved.

### Research set

| Reference | Source type | Relevant recurring principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Proko: 7 Ways Simple Shapes Can Improve Your Drawings](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/7-ways-simple-shapes-can-improve-your-drawings) | Popular drawing education | Simplification is an intentional design process: establish large recognizable shapes, judge proportion early, and let later detail support rather than obscure them. |
| [Toon Boom Learn: Line and Shape Language](https://learn.toonboom.com/modules/background-design/topic/line-and-shape-language) | Professional animation training | Environment line direction, dominant shapes, and color choices can establish mood and support a scene's story. |
| [BINUS University: Worldbuilding in 2D Animation](https://binus.ac.id/bandung/dkv/2025/11/04/worldbuilding-in-2d-animation-designing-an-animated-universe/) | University design education | A coherent animated world applies a consistent visual language across shape, color, props, and environment rather than stylizing each object independently. |
| [University of Greenwich: Shape Theory in Animation Pedagogy and Practice](https://gala.gre.ac.uk/id/eprint/46650/) | Academic research | Shape associations are useful, culturally developed conventions rather than objective laws; they should be applied deliberately and critically. |
| [Library of Congress: Political Cartoons and Public Debates](https://www.loc.gov/classroom-materials/political-cartoons-and-public-debates/) | Public archive and classroom education | Cartoons communicate through selective emphasis, symbol, caricature, exaggeration, and clear point of view. |
| [Billy Ireland Cartoon Library & Museum: Motion Lines](https://library.osu.edu/exhibits/motion-lines-how-cartoonists-draw-movement) | University museum and archive | Cartoonists use an economical visual vocabulary—including directional lines, exaggeration, repetition, and visible paths—to suggest energy in a static image. |

The set spans popular instruction, professional training, university education,
academic research, and public museum/archive sources. The synthesis avoids treating
one production tradition as the definition of cartooning.

### Style synthesis matrix

| Dimension | Synthesized guidance for this guide |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Build the scene from a few readable masses and a strong directional path. Let the environment communicate a mood before small props appear. |
| Construction | Start from boxes, wedges, arcs, and foliage envelopes, then deliberately bend, compress, or enlarge selected relationships. Preserve a consistent internal rule set. |
| Perspective | Use believable spatial cues as a base, then simplify or gently warp convergence consistently for clarity and energy. Do not allow random structural errors to masquerade as stylization. |
| Line | Choose a coherent line behavior. A flat animation treatment favors clean controlled contours; a hand-inked treatment favors pressure changes, taper, interruption, and directional energy. |
| Shape | Prioritize silhouette and dominant shape rhythm. Repeat related curves or angles across buildings, stairs, vegetation, and clouds, while retaining enough contrast for recognition. |
| Value | Favor a small number of clearly separated value groups. Use contrast to direct attention rather than fully model every surface. |
| Color | Use a limited, intentional palette with distinct color families and one or two accents. Color organizes mood and hierarchy more than observed local variation. |
| Texture | Keep texture subordinate to the dominant shapes. Flat treatments use sparse graphic patterns; hand-inked treatments use selective hatching and dry-brush variation. |
| Detail | Remove incidental information, enlarge identifying features, and distribute details unevenly around the focal path. Every retained detail should support recognition, rhythm, or story. |
| Medium | Clean opaque digital color and expressive ink-with-color are both valid Cartoon branches. The medium should reinforce the chosen edge and shape behavior. |
| Workflow | Thumbnail the mass design, choose a shape rule, construct the simplified scene, push selected proportions, test readability at small size, then add limited value, color, and accents. |

### Candidate outcome

- The approved showcase keeps Candidate A's bold flat color, rounded shape rhythm,
  compressed architecture, and clear contour hierarchy.
- It selectively borrows Candidate B's varied contour pressure, small asymmetries,
  sparse ink texture, and focal contrast without adopting its dense sketch finish.

## Architectural drawing

Status: the axonometric candidate is approved as the Architectural drawing showcase.
Its vertically organized learner reference and process sheet are also approved.

### Research set

| Reference | Source type | Relevant recurring principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [U.S. National Park Service: HABS Drawing Guidelines](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/heritagedocumentation/upload/HABS-Guidelines-Measured-Drawings_508.pdf) | Government heritage-documentation standard | Measured drawings prioritize reproducible clarity, consistent conventions, visible evidence, restrained dimensioning, and relationships that can be checked. |
| [University of Memphis: Architectural Drawing Standards](https://www.memphis.edu/architecture/docs/drawing.standards.pdf) | University architecture program | Heavy, medium, light, and very-light lines communicate cut, edge, surface, texture, and depth hierarchy rather than decoration. |
| [New York City College of Technology: Fundamentals of Perspective](https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/arch1110fall2013/files/2011/06/Fundamentals-of-Perspective.pdf) | University course material | Architectural perspective combines geometric construction with deliberate composition and legible spatial communication. |
| [RIBA: The Homes We Build](https://www.riba.org/media/wsfmr4lv/the-homes-we-build-ks2-learning-resource.pdf) | Professional institute education | Plan, section, and elevation are distinct projections that reveal different spatial and construction relationships. |
| [Autodesk: Architectural Drawing](https://www.autodesk.com/industry/architecture/architectural-drawing/) | Professional software and industry education | Architectural drawing is a technical visual language spanning sketches, measured views, and coordinated project representations. |
| [Creative Bloq: Isometric Drawing—A Designer's Guide](https://www.creativebloq.com/features/isometric-drawing) | Popular professional instruction | Isometric drawing keeps a common scale on its axes, making volume and spatial relationships readable without perspective convergence. |

The set spans government standards, university teaching, a professional institute,
industry education, and popular professional instruction. The guide uses recurring
projection and hierarchy principles without reproducing any source drawing.

### Style synthesis matrix

| Dimension | Synthesized guidance for this guide |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Choose the projection that best explains the intended relationship. Perspective emphasizes experience and depth; axonometric emphasizes organization and comparability. |
| Construction | Establish horizon and vanishing points for perspective, or a consistent parallel-axis system for axonometric. Build terrain and buildings from aligned planes and volumes. |
| Perspective | Never mix converging perspective and parallel projection accidentally. Any deliberate departure must remain consistent across the entire drawing. |
| Line | Use a small hierarchy: strongest profile or cut edges, medium primary edges, light surface divisions, and very light guides or texture. Line weight carries information. |
| Shape | Preserve dimensional and structural relationships. Simplify repeated roof, window, stair, and vegetation shapes without obscuring how components meet. |
| Value | Use restrained poche, cast shadow, or tonal wash to separate planes and depth. Tone supports line hierarchy rather than replacing it. |
| Color | Color is optional and informational. A limited palette may distinguish terrain, built form, water, circulation, or sun/shade while retaining drawing legibility in monochrome. |
| Texture | Use conventional or simplified material hatching selectively and at a lighter weight than profiles. Avoid texture that muddies dimensions or spatial edges. |
| Detail | Include details that clarify scale, construction, circulation, or material change. Omit incidental realism that does not explain the design. |
| Medium | Precise ink/graphite drafting with restrained wash or pencil color can remain visibly handmade while honoring geometric discipline. |
| Workflow | Select projection, establish axes, block primary volumes and terrain, verify alignments, apply line hierarchy, add restrained tone/color, then annotate or texture only where useful. |

### Candidate outcome

- The perspective candidate remains an unselected preview demonstrating experiential
  depth and visible vanishing-point construction.
- The approved axonometric cutaway uses parallel axes, a heavy site-block profile,
  exposed terrain strata, continuous stair circulation, and restrained system color
  to make the hillside's vertical organization feel textbook-clear.

## Watercolor

Status: the loose atmospheric wet-on-wet candidate is approved as the Watercolor
showcase. Its reduced-wash learner reference and process sheet are also approved.

### Research set

| Reference | Source type | Relevant recurring principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Watercolor](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/materials-and-techniques-drawing-watercolor) | Museum materials education | Transparent pigment over white paper creates luminosity; damp paper, layered washes, and drying behavior produce distinct diffuse edges, tide lines, and color modifications. |
| [MoMA: A Guide to Cézanne's Mark-Making and Materials](https://www.moma.org/magazine/articles/589) | Museum conservation and interpretation | Watercolor brilliance depends on transparent washes and visible paper; blank paper, fragmented contours, crisp dry strokes, and layered veils can coexist. |
| [Transparent Watercolor Society of America: Transparency](https://www.watercolors.org/transparency) | Professional watercolor society | Even dark or nominally opaque pigments can function transparently when paper texture and value variation remain visible through the application. |
| [University of Saskatchewan: Watercolor I](https://artsandscience.usask.ca/noncredit/communityarts/classes/classpages/2026d/watercolour-i-afternoon-colleen.php) | University community-art education | Flat, graded, and variegated washes; wet-on-wet, wet-on-dry, dry brush, hard and soft edges, and glazing form a practical core vocabulary. |
| [Winsor & Newton: Watercolor technique guide](https://www.winsornewton.com/en-ca/blogs/how-tos/tate-sargent-watercolour-set) | Materials manufacturer education | Water amount and drying stage control dispersion, later thicker marks, glazing, layering, and expressive final accents. |
| [Getty: Watercolor Techniques](https://www.getty.edu/education/teachers/classroom_resources/tips_tools/downloads/watercolor_techniques.pdf) | Museum classroom education | A study can combine broad washes with controlled brush effects and experimentation while remaining attentive to the paper and transparency. |

The set spans museum conservation and teaching, a professional society, university
instruction, and materials education. The guide synthesizes shared medium behavior
rather than imitating a particular watercolorist.

### Style synthesis matrix

| Dimension | Synthesized guidance for this guide |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Plan the large light shapes and reserved paper before the first wash. Let linked wash areas create a readable flow rather than outlining every object. |
| Construction | Use a light economical drawing to place horizon, architecture, stair direction, and major foliage. Construction must survive even when later edges soften. |
| Perspective | Establish believable spatial anchors, then use overlaps, value steps, edge softness, and pigment strength to reinforce distance. |
| Line | Line may be absent, lightly penciled, selectively inked, or reintroduced with a brush. Avoid uniformly enclosing every wash shape. |
| Shape | Group adjacent objects into large connected wash shapes. Preserve a few decisive silhouettes and negative spaces while allowing secondary boundaries to merge. |
| Value | Reserve paper for the brightest lights and build darks through planned transparent layers. Maintain a small readable value structure before adding chromatic nuance. |
| Color | Allow optical mixing through glazing and controlled mingling on paper. Limit pigment families to avoid mud and repeat mixtures for unity. |
| Texture | Use granulation, dry brush, blooms, broken paper, and tide edges intentionally and selectively. Medium artifacts should describe atmosphere or material, not cover mistakes indiscriminately. |
| Detail | Delay details until washes are dry or nearly dry. Add the few sharp, dark accents that locate the focal area and leave other passages unresolved. |
| Medium | Water-to-pigment ratio, paper wetness, brush load, absorbency, and drying time determine edge and transparency behavior as much as the chosen color. |
| Workflow | Reserve lights, establish a light drawing, lay broad pale washes, connect major mid-value shapes, dry and glaze selectively, then finish with limited dry-brush or line accents. |

### Candidate outcome

- The approved showcase uses Candidate A's loose atmospheric wet-on-wet washes,
  merged and lost edges, reserved paper, granulation, and a small number of decisive
  dark accents.
- Candidate B remains an unselected preview of a structure-first line-and-wash branch
  with visible ink, controlled local-color washes, and dry-brush detail.

## Anime environment art

Status: a new graphic cliffside-town direction, developed after review of the initial
candidate pair and a user-supplied visual reference, is approved as the Anime
environment showcase. Its simplified learner reference and process sheet are also
approved.

### Research set

| Reference | Source type | Relevant recurring principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Animation World Network: Animation Layout—Tonal Studies](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/animation-layout-tonal-studies) | Professional animation publication | Tonal planning precedes color and establishes readable staging, depth, emphasis, and the bridge from layout drawing to final background. |
| [Toon Boom Learn: Composition Principles](https://learn.toonboom.com/modules/background-design/topic/composition-principles) | Professional animation training | Background clarity relies on solid composition, foreground/midground/background planes, readable silhouettes, controlled tangents, and focal contrast in value or color. |
| [Toon Boom Learn: Background Layout](https://learn.toonboom.com/modules/background-layout-with-storyboard-pro) | Professional production training | One-, two-, and three-point perspective guides support precise layouts that can be handed through a production pipeline. |
| [D'Source / IIT Bombay: Layout Design for Animation](https://www.dsource.in/course/layout-design-animation-part-ii/production) | University design education | Production layouts separate foreground, overlay, underlay, and background so staging, effects, depth, and potential camera movement remain clear. |
| [Philippines Department of Education: Digital Animation Skills Framework](https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/PSF-Digital-Animation.pdf) | Government skills framework | Background production begins from story and concept requirements, researches setting, develops mood through lighting and color, undergoes review, and maintains a prescribed visual language. |
| [DF TALK: Background Expression in Anime Art](https://note.com/dftalk/n/nf5607e2d8a80?hl=en) | Industry practitioner interview | Background artists test color and atmosphere, deliberately remove information, and adjust perspective and framing to the camera and narrative purpose. |
| [Gnomon: Designing Backgrounds for Animation](https://dart.thegnomonworkshop.com/tutorials/designing-backgrounds-for-animation) | Professional art education | A production background develops from script and thumbnails through selected layout, color studies, and a clean final usable by the larger production. |

The set spans trade publication, production training, university teaching, a public
skills framework, professional education, and practitioner discussion. No studio,
artist, production, or existing image is treated as the visual target.

### Style synthesis matrix

| Dimension | Synthesized guidance for this guide |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Stage a clear focal area and leave usable visual space. Organize foreground, middle distance, and background as purposeful production layers rather than equally detailed scenery. |
| Construction | Build a layout from camera position, horizon, perspective grid, terrain planes, architecture, and overlays before painting. Keep the setting reusable and internally coherent. |
| Perspective | Use deliberate lens and viewpoint choices to control scale and emotion. Maintain consistent convergence, but simplify or widen spatial relationships when staging requires clarity. |
| Line | Layout lines solve geometry and placement; final paint may absorb most lines. Retain only selective structural or material edges when they support the chosen background tradition. |
| Shape | Design recognizable building, roof, foliage, cloud, and shadow masses that read quickly. Simplify secondary information and avoid tangents where subjects would need to animate. |
| Value | Establish a tonal plan before color. Separate depth planes, protect focal contrast, and ensure the setting remains readable even without hue. |
| Color | Use a limited color script to establish time, weather, emotion, and focal hierarchy. Repeat key colors across planes and temper distant saturation and contrast. |
| Texture | Concentrate tactile paint or digital texture around foreground and story-bearing materials. Quiet staging areas and distance so texture does not compete with potential action. |
| Detail | Use selective lived-in evidence—wear, repairs, plants, utilities, and small objects—to imply habitation. Remove detail that does not support place, mood, scale, or staging. |
| Medium | Traditional opaque background paint and layered digital painting are both valid. Each should retain its native edge, brush, and compositing logic rather than emulate photography. |
| Workflow | Interpret story intent, thumbnail camera and masses, verify perspective layout, create tonal and color studies, paint by depth planes, add selective story detail, and review at final frame size. |

### Candidate outcome

- The initial tactile and cinematic candidates were both too close to naturalistic
  environment painting for the user's intended category.
- The approved direction uses crisp painted shapes, decisive five-value grouping,
  deep teal shadow masses, bright yellow-green foliage, simplified faceted rock,
  strong vertical cliff staging, and selective structural line accents.
- A user-supplied image informed only broad preferences for verticality, a warm lived-in
  building, dense trees, rock, and moving water. The final uses an original cliffside
  inn, support system, watercourse, path network, viewpoint, rock silhouette, lighting,
  and color organization rather than reproducing the supplied composition or motifs.
